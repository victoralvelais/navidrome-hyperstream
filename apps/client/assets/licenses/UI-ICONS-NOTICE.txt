MIT notice appears separately in this app's open-source licenses.

Bundled SVGs are selected from the pinned Iconify packages recorded in
tools/ui_icons_sources.json. Hyperstream normalizes SVG wrappers and colors for
theme tinting, fills Lucide transport/active symbols and recording dots, and
selects equivalent actions across families. Solar's original opacity is retained.
